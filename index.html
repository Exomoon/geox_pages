GeoX Mars Explorer

Planetary terrain & geospatial analysis laboratory for Mars.

Interactive 3D WebGL engine combining NASA MOLA global elevation data (16 ppd), USGS/CTX imagery, and high-resolution HiRISE/CTX terrain packages. Features mission traverse data for Perseverance and Curiosity, cross-sectional transects, and paleocean flooding simulations.

3D WebGL Terrain NASA MOLA 16 PPD CTX 5m & HiRISE DTM Rover Traverses Topographic Transects Cloudflare Pages

Sentinel Lab

High-throughput Copernicus Sentinel-2 MSI Level-2A processing.

Automated extraction of 10-meter Bottom-of-Atmosphere (BOA) reflectance, Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI), burn severity (dNBR), and water indices directly from European Copernicus satellite acquisitions.

Capabilities

Technical focus areas across planetary altimetry, spatial modeling, data pipelines, and digital interfaces.

Planetary & Elevation Modeling

NASA MOLA datum, USGS/CTX orthomosaics, HiRISE sub-meter DTMs, hypsometric ramps, and 3D terrain rendering.

Multispectral Satellite Pipelines

Automated Copernicus Sentinel-2 MSI Level-2A processing, Bottom-of-Atmosphere (BOA) reflectance, NDVI, and dNBR.

Geospatial Engineering

Coordinate reference systems, vector tiling, spatial indexing, geodesic calculations, and geometric transformations.

Data Pipelines & Spatial ETL

Automated ingestion, schema normalization, batch processing, and reproducible pipelines for complex datasets.

Spatial & Isochrone Analysis

Multimodal routing, accessibility modeling, travel-time catchment areas, and network disruption scenarios.

Interactive WebGL & Mapping

Ultra-fast client-side WebGL and canvas renderers designed for smooth exploration without server GPU overhead.
